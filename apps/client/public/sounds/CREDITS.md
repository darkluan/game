# Sound credits

Every file in this folder is released under Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0): free for personal and
commercial use, credit not required.

| Files                                                                                     | Pack                                  | Source                                               |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| `chop`, `knifeSlice*`                                                                     | RPG sounds, by Kenney (www.kenney.nl) | https://opengameart.org/content/50-rpg-sound-effects |
| `blade_*`, `clang_*`, `creature_roar_*`, `spell_*`, `stones_*`, `chain_*`, `item_coins_*` | 80 CC0 RPG SFX, by rubberduck         | https://opengameart.org/content/80-cc0-rpg-sfx       |
| `hit_*`, `shot_*`, `explosion`, `slam_*`, `tools_*`, `machine_01`                         | 100 CC0 SFX, by rubberduck            | https://opengameart.org/content/100-cc0-sfx          |

The files were converted from Ogg to MP3 (mono, 96 kbit/s), levelled to the same peak and cut to at
most 2.2 seconds. `clang_*` are the pack's `metal_*` files, renamed.
