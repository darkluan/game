## Quaternius

The dinosaurs (the dragon heroes and their evolved forms) and the mechs (the machine heroes) are by
Quaternius (quaternius.com), also released under CC0 1.0. They were downloaded from the
quaternius-showcase project, which hosts each model as a separate file.

| Files                                                                                                          | Pack                   | Source                                              |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| `velociraptor.glb`, `parasaurolophus.glb`, `trex.glb`, `triceratops.glb`, `stegosaurus.glb`, `apatosaurus.glb` | Animated Dinosaur Pack | https://quaternius.com/packs/animateddinosaurs.html |
| `mech_mike.glb`, `mech_leela.glb`, `mech_stan.glb`                                                             | Animated Mech Pack     | https://quaternius.com/packs/animatedmech.html      |

The mech files were reduced: unused animations were removed and the texture was scaled down to
512 pixels. The dinosaur files are unchanged.
